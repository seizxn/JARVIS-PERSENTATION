01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

ONE ASSISTANT.
MORE WAYS TO WORK.

Most tasks still rely on separate apps and manual input. We built J.A.R.V.I.S. to bring information, useful tools, spoken commands, and hand gestures into one system.

The system recognizes spoken instructions, not just questions. It can respond to supported requests such as opening websites, searching, and starting timers. Available actions depend on the platform and permissions.
